DATA PROVENANCE / 06

Sources & methods

What we know, what we infer and what we absolutely cannot claim.

01

IODA · Georgia Tech

Country-level alerts based on independent internet measurement signals. Alerts do not prove root cause, completeness or nationwide loss of access.

IODA OFFICIAL ↗
02

Official status pages

Cloudflare, GitHub, Vercel and OpenAI publish their own service statuses. These are not independent measures of the entire internet.

CLOUDFLARE ↗GITHUB ↗VERCEL ↗OPENAI ↗
03

RIPEstat · RIPE NCC

Registry resources by country, such as ASNs and IP resources. Allocation inventory is not a count of active networks or an indicator of performance.

RIPESTAT DOCUMENTATION ↗
04

Coverage, freshness & uncertainty

A source may be delayed, rate-limited or unavailable. Unknown never means healthy. Demo records are explicitly simulated. Route explorer and labs are educational models only. Server cache is approximately 2 minutes.
